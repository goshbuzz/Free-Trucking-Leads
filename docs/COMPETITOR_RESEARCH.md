# Positioning notes

Reviewed November 27, 2026. Competitor prices and features can change; compare like for like before using pricing claims in advertising.

- [XDate Alert](https://xdatealert.com/) lists a $39/month solo plan for insurance leads across its covered states, with daily emails and a dashboard.
- [PollyAI](https://getpollyai.com/blog/best-trucking-insurance-leads) describes a $39/month base plus $40/month per state bundle and includes sales-workflow features.
- [Apify FMCSA carrier feed](https://apify.com/technicaldost/fmcsa-trucking-sales-triggers) describes pay-per-result feeds and references another carrier feed at $2 per 1,000. Compare products, billing periods and features before making pricing claims.
- [New Trucking Companies comparison](https://newtruckingcompanies.com/compare/) lists new-authority vendors and their delivery options; it is a competitor's comparison and should not be treated as an independent audit.

Proposed positioning: **accessible self-serve carrier research, bringing new-business and renewal opportunities together with useful filters and CSV export**. The planned entry prices are simple, but price alone is not a defensible claim to be the cheapest. Other products offer alerts, integrations and workflow features this app does not currently provide. The paid tiers and usage limits are not live. Insurance effective dates are not proof of a new authority grant; communicate this distinction.
